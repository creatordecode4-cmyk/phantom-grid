# Building notes (written before coding)

These notes describe the reference pictures in `assets/reference/buildings/`
and how each building is rebuilt from code. Every picture shows ONE building
in four views (front, left, back, right). There are no `*_clay.png` shape
pictures in the folder, so the volumes below were read from the color pictures.

Scale used everywhere: 1 unit = 1 m, one floor = 4 m.

## What all references have in common (the design language)

- **Sculpted forms.** Rounded floor plans (rounded rectangles, ovals), bodies that
  twist, taper or pinch in the middle, floors that step back into terraces.
  Nothing is a plain box.
- **Ribbon frames.** A wide light-grey band (about 2 to 4 m wide, 0.3 to 0.6 m
  thick) sweeps over the facade in S-curves and wraps around corners. The fields
  next to a ribbon are often filled with **perforated metal** (small dots).
- **Glass curtain walls.** Dark blue-grey tinted glass, thin floor lines every
  4 m, dense upright fins, warm lit rooms in some windows.
- **Green.** Planters with hanging plants on terraces, full-height green walls,
  real trees on the roofs.
- **Roofs.** Round helipad or drone pad on a short stem, blue solar panels,
  sometimes a thin overhanging roof plate. One rooftop pool (b06).
- **Sky bridges.** Glass box walkways with a light truss, sticking out of the
  building at one floor (they connect to the neighbour).
- **Ground floor.** Glass lobby (podium) with warm light, EV charging posts or
  EV bays with a green light strip, ramps.
- Materials: light concrete, brushed light-grey metal, graphite metal, tinted
  glass, perforated metal, warm wood slats, plants. Slight dirt, no toy look.

## The buildings

### b01 twist tower - about 40 floors, 160 m (The Crown; 260 m version = Kessler-Vance HQ)
- Shape: rounded-square glass tower that **twists** about 80 degrees from the
  bottom to the top and is a little **narrower in the middle** (waist at half
  height). Two-floor **green podium**, wider than the tower, on a concrete plaza
  with ramps.
- Special parts: two big **diagonal ribbons** wrap around the tower like a
  helix and cross each other, so "eye" shaped fields open between them. Inside
  the eyes: dark glass with warm windows. Outside: a light perforated metal
  veil. A diagonal band of **sky garden terraces** (8 to 10 floors with plants)
  follows one ribbon. **Two projecting platforms**: a glass sky-bridge stub at
  about 55% height and a garden balcony at about 40%. Top: roof garden with
  trees, **helipad** disc, tilted **solar panels** on the top floors.
- Materials: blue-grey glass, light-grey ribbons, perforated veil, concrete base.

### b02 terrace tower - about 20 floors, 80 m
- Shape: rounded rectangle, two stacked blocks (floors 1-9 and 11-20) with an
  open **sky garden floor** between them (columns, lots of green).
- One half of every side is smooth glass with dense fins and a round corner,
  the other half is **open terraces**: a white floor plate with planter and
  hanging plants on every floor.
- Special parts: wide perforated **ribbon frames** outline the glass half and
  swing across the facade in an S. A **glass sky bridge** leaves at the garden
  floor. Roof: trees, a **drone pad** on a round stem, a small solar field.
- Ground: glass lobby with warm light, ramp, EV chargers.

### b03 garden midrise - 7 floors, 30 m
- Shape: rounded square, three thick "trays": every second floor a deep
  concrete terrace ring full of plants. A thin roof plate hangs over the top.
- Special parts: a **big green wall** two tiers high, **perforated curved
  panels** between the ribbon and the trays, a ribbon that swings diagonally
  over each tier, **solar roof with a drone**, trees on the roof, an **outdoor
  glass stair** from the street to the first tray, a **sky bridge** at the top tier.
- Glass with dense upright fins, EV posts at the door.

### b04 office hub - 8 floors, 35 m
- Shape: rounded block on a dark recessed ground floor with **EV bays** (cars
  behind green-lit frames). Above it a **curved glass podium** two to three
  floors high, framed like one huge rounded window. Upper part: one half
  stepped green terraces, the other half a rounded perforated volume with fins.
- Special parts: ribbon from the podium up to the roof, a wide thin **roof
  wing**, a **helipad disc** on a stem, solar panels, a long **sky bridge** to
  the twin tower at mid height.

### b05 villa cluster - 4 to 5 stepped villas, 20 m
- Shape: each villa is 4 to 5 floors of rounded trays that **step back**, so
  every floor has a garden terrace. They stand on a rounded **concrete plinth**.
- Materials: graphite metal with upright slats, **warm wood** walls, warm
  windows, a thin warm light line along the tray edges.
- Special parts: rooftop gardens with trees, a solar roof on the top box, a
  **helipad** mark on one terrace, garages with cars, **thin bridges** from
  villa to villa.

### b06 beach hotel - 12 floors, 50 m (Neon Mile)
- Shape: oval tower. Every floor has a thick white **wave-shaped balcony** band
  (the band swings in and out as it goes round). Wider curved base with
  arch-like legs.
- Special parts: **rooftop pool** at the roof edge with a deck and trees, a
  penthouse block with a **helipad** on top, green walls and two ribbons on the
  back, a **sky bridge** at about floor 7, landscaped plinth with ramps and EV
  parking.
- Dark glass with fins behind the balconies, warm rooms.

### b07 drone and EV hub - 6 floors, 35 m (plus a 55 m tower)
- Shape: rounded block with a tall glass front and white fins, on a concrete
  ground floor with **garage doors** and a row of **EV chargers**.
- Special parts: a **wavy dark metal roof** (standing seams, white edge) with
  trees, a ribbon that cuts diagonally over the front with **stepped green
  terraces** above it, green walls at the back, and a slim **drone tower**
  (55 m) with a **solar strip** on its face, zig-zag metal stairs and a landing
  deck with a big drone on top.

### b08 retrofit brick tower - 14 floors, 55 m (The Grid)
- Shape: an **old brick tower** (about 9 floors, dark red weathered brick, tall
  arched bays, small windows) that got new parts: a **glass volume** with
  balconies set into one corner and rising above the brick, a grey metal box on
  top, bronze trim lines.
- Special parts: a bronze **rounded frame** around the glass part with an S
  step where it cuts into the brick, upright **wood/bronze slats**, **green
  walls** and hanging plants, **two helipads**, solar panels, aerials, trees on
  the roof, a **sky bridge** near the top, EV garages at the street.

### b10 S-curve tower - about 45 floors, 180 m (The Crown, second landmark)
- Shape: tall rounded-rectangle glass tower, strong floor lines. The top
  eighth is a slightly smaller glass "crown box".
- Special parts: **one huge white S-shaped ribbon** from the foot to the top;
  it ends in a tall curved fin that rises above the roof. The field inside the
  S is a lighter veil with upright lines. **Sky gardens** cut into the tower at
  about 30% and 60% height, roof garden, **helipad** on a round core, **two
  sky bridges** at about 35% and 47% height. Glass podium with a white canopy.

### b11 hourglass tower - about 35 floors, 140 m (The Crown and Neon Mile)
- Shape: rounded-square glass tower that gets **narrower toward the middle**
  (waist at about 55% height) and wider again at the top. White floor plates
  show on every floor.
- Special parts: a dark blue **solar strip** (about 3 m wide, white frame) runs
  up the facade in a soft S. Garden notches at about 45% and 62% height, the top
  four floors step back with terraces and trees, a **helipad disc** hangs out
  at the top. Concrete ground floor with upright fins and EV chargers.

### b09 Neon Mile arcade - 3 floors, 12 m (no picture, designed from the brief)
- Long low building with a **curved glass front**, shops behind it with soft
  lit signs, a thin **floating canopy roof** on slim columns that hangs over
  the sidewalk, a **green wall** at one end, a roof garden, EV chargers at the
  street.

## How they are built (kit of parts, `src/city/buildingKit.js`)

| Part | How it is made |
|---|---|
| Tower body | a stack of rounded-rectangle rings; each ring can be scaled, shifted and turned, which gives twisted, tapered and hourglass towers |
| Glass curtain wall | the body skin with a glass shader: floor lines, mullions, tinted reflecting glass, warm lit rooms at night |
| Fins | instanced thin boxes on the glass, only near the player |
| Rounded floor slabs | instanced slab rings (one per floor), also the wave balconies |
| Ribbon frame | a band swept along a curve on the body surface |
| Perforated panel, wood slats, seam roof, solar, pool | one "panel" material that paints these patterns |
| Terrace, hanging garden | slab arc + planter hedge + instanced leaf clusters and vines |
| Green wall | wall patch with a leaf texture + leaf clusters |
| Helipad, drone pad, sky bridge, EV bay, solar field | small instanced models |
| Old brick (retrofit) | the brick wall with 3D windows from the earlier version |

Level of detail: the body skin and the ribbons are always drawn (cheap). Fins,
slab rings, plants and small parts are instanced and only drawn near the
player (about 150 m). Far towers of The Crown use the body skin only.
